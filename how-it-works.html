How Releaf works

Releaf is planned as a simple way to discover a DIY idea, understand its cost, make it and record the result.

Discover → Calculate → Make → Track

This is the four-step process shown in the Releaf presentation.

  1. Discover

    Find a DIY recipe that matches your needs and the materials you can access.

  2. Calculate

    Review the ingredients and estimated cost. Compare the DIY cost with a store price when reliable prices are available.

  3. Make

    Follow the step-by-step instructions and use materials carefully.

  4. Track

    Record the money saved and the waste you helped reduce. This page explains the idea; it does not currently store data.
