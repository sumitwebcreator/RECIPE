Bottle Planter

Give a clean, used plastic bottle another purpose by turning it into a small planter.

Materials you may need

Clean plastic bottle, soil, a small plant, marker, scissors and water.

Ask an adult for help when cutting the bottle. Smooth or cover sharp edges.

Steps

  1. Wash the bottle and remove its label.
  2. Mark the area where the opening will be made.
  3. Ask an adult to cut the bottle safely.
  4. Make small drainage holes if the design needs them.
  5. Add suitable soil and place the small plant inside.
  6. Water lightly and keep the planter in a suitable place.

Cost and reuse

The cost depends on the plant and soil you choose. Reusing a bottle can reduce the need to buy a new container.
