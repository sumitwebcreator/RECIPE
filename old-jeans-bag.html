Old Jeans Bag

Reuse a pair of old jeans by turning suitable denim fabric into a simple carry bag.

Materials you may need

Old jeans, strong thread, needle, scissors, measuring tape and handles or denim strips.

This project involves sewing. Use needles and scissors carefully and ask for help if needed.

Steps

  1. Choose a clean pair of jeans that is no longer needed.
  2. Decide the size and shape of the bag.
  3. Cut the denim pieces with enough extra fabric for seams.
  4. Join the sides and bottom by sewing them securely.
  5. Make handles from strong denim strips or another suitable material.
  6. Attach the handles firmly and check the seams before use.

Cost and reuse

Using old jeans can reduce the need to buy new fabric. The final cost depends on thread, handles and any extra materials.
