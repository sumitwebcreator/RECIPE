Paper Organizer

Reuse old cardboard and paper to create a simple organizer for your desk.

Materials you may need

Clean cardboard, old paper, ruler, pencil, glue and scissors.

Use scissors carefully and ask for help if you need to cut thick cardboard.

Steps

  1. Collect clean cardboard from an old box.
  2. Decide how many sections you need for your desk items.
  3. Measure and mark the cardboard pieces.
  4. Cut the pieces carefully to make the base and sides.
  5. Join the pieces with glue and allow them to dry.
  6. Cover the organizer with reused paper if you want to decorate it.

Cost and reuse

You may be able to use cardboard and paper already available at home. Extra cost depends on glue and decoration materials.
